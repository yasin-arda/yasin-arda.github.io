01 About

Creative thinking. Clear execution.

I come from a graphic design background, so I pay close attention to detail, impact and visual clarity. At the same time, I want to understand the purpose behind a task: Who is it for? What problem should it solve? And how can the idea be implemented in a meaningful way?

02 How I approach work

Design is more than a polished surface.

Good design needs to be clear, fit the task and work in practice.

I prefer not to jump straight into design. First, I want to understand what the task needs to achieve, which information matters and what the final use requires.

From there, I make decisions about structure, content, imagery and execution. Not everything needs to be eye-catching, but every choice should have a reason.

01

Clarity

Organise information and reduce it to what matters.

02

03

04
