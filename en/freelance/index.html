Common questions

Clarity before the first project.

If your task does not fit neatly into one category, a short email with the context is enough.

How much does a project cost?

After a short briefing, you receive a quote based on scope, timing and the required outcome. I avoid generic packages for work that is not genuinely comparable.

Which files will I receive?

By default, you receive the agreed final files, such as PDF, PNG or JPG. Editable source files and licences for fonts or stock assets are only included when agreed explicitly.

How are revisions handled?

Design projects normally include two consolidated feedback rounds. Additional work or a fundamental change of direction is discussed before it is carried out.

Can you help at short notice?

Often, depending on scope and current workload. Cross-time-zone collaboration and urgent requests are possible by agreement.

Do you provide full SEO or advertising campaigns?

I can support SEO basics, content, research and implementation. I do not currently position myself as a senior technical SEO or performance marketing consultant.
