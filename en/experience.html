PACKAGING

Precision & Production

Design that has to work in production.

At EURES, my focus shifted towards product packaging, technical requirements and precise execution for production.

Packaging had to work visually while reliably meeting specific customer and manufacturer requirements. Final artwork, variants, image retouching, mock-ups and production-ready files therefore became a central part of my work.

Workflow at EURES

From requirements to a production-ready file.

Packaging was primarily about precision and process: understanding requirements, executing designs accurately, visualising results and preparing reliable production files.

  1. 01
    Understand requirements

    Review customer and manufacturer specifications and assess technical requirements accurately.

  2. 02
    Execute the design

    Develop new layouts or adapt existing designs precisely to formats, variants and specifications.

  3. 03
    Visualise the result

    Retouch images and create mock-ups and 3D visualisations so the design and product can be evaluated before production.

  4. 04
    Prepare for production

    Final artwork, final checks and delivery of clean production-ready files.
