Training

My professional training in Digital Marketing.

Since March 2026, I have been expanding my design experience through a full-time Digital Marketing training programme at WBS TRAINING.

The programme covers 1,210 teaching units and runs until 8 September 2026. Topics range from customer journeys, branding and e-commerce through lead management and data-driven marketing to web development, CMS, SEO and SEA.

Goal of the training

Understanding how digital processes fit together.

For me, this is not about listing as many individual tools as possible. I want to understand how target audiences, content, design, technology and marketing activities work together in digital projects.

This knowledge helps me place tasks in a broader context and make better-informed decisions.
