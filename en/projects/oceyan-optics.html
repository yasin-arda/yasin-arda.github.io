Project 04 OCEYAN Optics

Storytelling · Copywriting · Content · 2026

← All projects

Case Study · Storytelling & Copywriting · Fictional Brand · AI-assisted visuals

Storytelling that turns product facts into a brand story.

For OCEYAN Optics, I developed a storytelling and copywriting concept across web, video and brand voice. The focus is on individuality, conscious consumption and recycled ocean plastic.

Focus Storytelling & Copywriting
Formats Website · Video · Brand Voice
Core idea

Individuality and responsibility do not have to be opposites.

01

The conflict

Product facts alone do not create a brand story.

Recycled material, 3D printing and an individual fit explain what OCEYAN does. But they do not yet tell a story.

The task was to connect product benefits, personal identity and sustainability in one coherent story.

Personal conflict Individuality versus Mass production
Social conflict Conscious consumption versus Sacrifice

02 · Narrative structure

The story follows a clear transformation.

OCEYAN itself is not at the centre of the story. The human perspective comes first, while the brand acts as a guide and offers a possible solution.

01 Exposition

Interchangeability

Products can feel short-lived and impersonal. At the same time, eyewear is visible every day and becomes part of personal identity.

02 Conflict

The plastic problem

The desire for more conscious consumption is set against resource use, plastic waste and the perception of sustainability as sacrifice.

03 Guide

OCEYAN enters the story

Collected ocean plastic is processed and turned into a custom-made eyewear frame using 3D printing.

04 Transformation

From material to a one-of-a-kind frame

A functional product becomes a personal object that connects fit, design and values.

05 Resolution

A conscious choice

The story does not end with the product, but with the person’s decision to connect individuality and responsibility.

03 · Web Storytelling

The brand story shapes the About page.

The five acts form the content structure of a digital About page, allowing the story to develop step by step along the user journey.

From feature to meaning

Product fact Recycled ocean plastic

→

Narrative meaning Something personal emerges from discarded material.

04 · From web to video story

The brand story as a 60-second video.

For Instagram Reels and TikTok, I developed the same core idea into a 60-second story. This included the narrative arc, scene structure, visual language, atmosphere and voice-over script.

60 seconds
5 scenes
1 Transformation
00–12 Familiar world

Interchangeable products and emotional distance.

12–24 The problem

Plastic waste is shown in a restrained, emotional way.

24–40 The guide

OCEYAN shows material processing and 3D printing.

40–54 Transformation

The eyewear becomes a form of personal expression.

54–60 Resolution & CTA

A small decision becomes a conscious statement.

05 · Brand Voice

A brand voice also needs to work when the conversation gets difficult.

Crisis communication

The brand should not dismiss or gloss over criticism of the energy use of 3D printing. Instead, the concept addresses energy use, on-demand production, overproduction and material choices openly and transparently.

Transparency instead of perfect sustainability claims.
